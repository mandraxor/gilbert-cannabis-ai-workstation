Interactive Tool Specifications
Tool 1: Vertical Risk & Coverage Gap Evaluator
Target Users: Dispensaries, Cultivators, Processors, Testing Labs, Landlords.

Input Parameters: State Domicile, Vertical Type, Square Footage, Annual Gross Revenue, On-Site Vault/Cash Holdings, Ancillary Service Providers.

Outputs: Interactive Risk Radar Map, Uninsured Gap Breakdown, Recommended Endorsements (Product Liability, Crop, Cyber, Excess Liability).

Vertical	Primary Threat Profile	Core Coverage Trigger	Gap Exposure Flag
Cultivation	HVAC/Power loss, crop mold, light cycles	Crop Insurance & Equipment Breakdown	Living plant vs. harvested inventory cutoff
Processing / Mfg	Solvents, extraction explosion, batch failure	Product Liability & Equipment Breakdown	Vendor component indemnification gaps
Dispensary	Inventory theft, cash liability, product claims	Property, General Liability, Cyber	Sub-limited cash/inventory transit terms
Landlord / Real Estate	Lease compliance, property damage, illegal activity	Property & Premises Excess Liability	Co-insurance penalties & forfeiture clauses
Tool 2: debido Vendor Risk Transfer Stress-Tester
Leveraging Gilbert’s proprietary debido Insurance Compliance framework.

Function: Evaluates the risk exposure created by third-party vendors (transporters, testing labs, raw material suppliers).

Interactive Logic: Checks vendor Certificates of Insurance (COI), primary/non-contributory wording, and additional insured endorsements across the supply chain.
